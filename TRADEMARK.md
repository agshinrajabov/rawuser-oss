# The name

"Rawuser" and its logo belong to PixelHurts, LLC. The licences in this
repository cover the code. They do not cover the name.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your site or your service runs Rawuser, when it runs this code
  unchanged or with changes that keep the five lines below.
- Write about Rawuser, compare it, teach it, and link to it by name.
- Name a package or a plugin "for Rawuser" when it works with Rawuser and
  is not Rawuser.

## What needs another name

- A changed version that you hand to other people as a product of its
  own. Call it something else and say it is based on Rawuser.
- Any version, changed or not, that does one of the things Rawuser
  promises never to do:

  1. fingerprints a visitor;
  2. carries an identity across sites or sets a third-party cookie;
  3. links one visitor's activity across customers;
  4. guesses a special category about a visitor (health, religion,
     politics, sexuality, ethnicity, union membership), or sorts visitors
     for prices, eligibility, job ads or admissions;
  5. sells or shares data about a single visitor.

  These are the lines of [docs/privacy-rules.md](docs/privacy-rules.md).
  The name stands for them. Software that crosses one is not Rawuser,
  and may not say it is.

## What this does and does not do

It keeps the name honest. It does not stop anyone from changing the code;
the licences allow that. So the name on a site is a claim by whoever runs
that site, and they answer for it. If you see the name on something that
crosses a line above, write to the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Do not

- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests PixelHurts, LLC runs,
  endorses or stands behind your service.
- Register the name, or one easily taken for it, as a domain, a company
  or a mark.

For anything this page does not answer, open an issue in this repository
before you use the name.
